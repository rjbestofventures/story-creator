# StoryCreator

StoryCreator.Bot interviews a business owner about their work and turns the answers into a library of publish-ready, episodic social content.

## Language

**Interview**:
The guided conversation in which StoryBot asks a business owner about their business and records their answers.
_Avoid_: chat, questionnaire, onboarding

**Story**:
One library of episodes generated from a single interview.
_Avoid_: series, campaign, project

**Episode**:
A single standalone piece of publish-ready content within a Story.
_Avoid_: post, article, entry

**Locked Episode**:
An Episode that has been fully written and stored, but which its owner may not read, edit, refine, or hear until it is unlocked. Locking withholds access to content that already exists.
_Avoid_: hidden, preview, teaser, draft

**Unlocking**:
Granting an owner access to a Locked Episode. Unlocking never writes or rewrites content — the Episode already exists in full.
_Avoid_: upgrading, revealing, generating

## Members

Two independent properties describe a member. They answer different questions and compose freely: a member may be either, both, or neither.

**Trial Member**:
A member whose generated Episodes arrive locked, and who generates without spending Credits. Answers the question "are this member's Episodes locked?" A temporary state, not a permanent property: it ends the moment the member acquires a main pack.
_Avoid_: free user, demo user, prospect, lead

**Complimentary Trial**:
A free, time-limited start for a prospect who signs up on the website: a small Credit allowance and one Story with most Episodes locked. It ends when the member buys a Retail Pack (which unlocks their Story), becomes a Verified Partner, or lets the clock run out.
_Avoid_: Temporary VBP, free trial, complementary trial

**Verified Partner**:
A member vetted off-platform who is offered partner pack pricing. Answers the question "what does this member pay?" — nothing else.
_Avoid_: reseller, affiliate, agency

**Credit**:
The unit a paying member spends to generate or refine one Episode. Trial Members hold none and spend none.
_Avoid_: token, episode credit, balance

**Trial Allowance**:
The number of Stories a Trial Member may still generate. Bounds the cost of a trial, since generation is free to them.
_Avoid_: quota, limit, remaining trials

## Packs

**Main Pack**:
A pack sold as a member's primary purchase, at either partner or retail pricing. Acquiring one ends a trial and sets the member's episode tier.
_Avoid_: plan, subscription, tier

**Retail Pack**:
A Main Pack bought on the platform by a member who is not a Verified Partner. Silver and Gold are the Retail Packs on sale; each matches the signup page's advertised price, Credits, and episode options exactly.
_Avoid_: storybot pack, pay-to-play plan, non-VBP plan

**VBP Plan**:
The plan (Silver, Gold, or Other) a Verified Partner is placed on off-platform. It grants Credits once, when the member becomes a partner, and sets their episode ceiling. Shares the names Silver and Gold with the Retail Packs but is never bought on the platform.
_Avoid_: partner pack, tier

**Add-on**:
A top-up pack sold only to a member who already holds a Main Pack. It grants Credits and nothing else — it never changes pricing, tier, or trial state.
_Avoid_: boost, extra, upgrade
